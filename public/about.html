WELCOME TO WILLOWBROOK

Agromate: a free cozy farming game for your browser.

A patch of earth, a handful of seeds, and a little place to call your own. Agromate is a single-player pixel-art farming game you can play on a desktop, tablet, or phone. No download, payment, or account needed.

What is Agromate?

At Willowbrook Farm, you prepare garden plots, sow seeds, water your crops, and harvest them when they are ready. Sell your produce to buy more seeds. End the day to rest and help watered crops grow.

The farm has a pond, a farmhouse, wandering chickens, ducks that fly in and paddle, and a tabby cat that strolls around catching mice. Kris the seagull visits from time to time; click or tap him and he says “hey hey.” Hover over your farmer or Kris to see their name.

How to play

  1. Enter your farmer’s name on the title screen and press Play. Returning players can Continue their saved farm.
  2. Choose the hoe (1) and click or tap a garden square to prepare the soil.
  3. Choose seeds (2) to open the seed selector. Pick the crop you want to sow, then click or tap prepared soil.
  4. Choose the watering can (3) to water each growing crop. End the day to grow watered crops and restore energy.
  5. Choose harvest (4) to collect ripe crops, then sell them with the basket (5) or the wooden shipping box.
  6. Buy more seeds at the Seed shop. The farm journal tracks your progress.

Keyboard, mouse, and touch controls

Move
WASD or arrow keys, click a path, or use the on-screen directional buttons on a phone.
Select tools
Press 1–5 or choose a tool in the toolbar.
Use a tool
Click or tap a garden tile, or press Space to use the selected tool in front of your farmer.
Choose seeds
Click the seed packet or press 2. Choose a crop in the popup; each type shows its remaining seed count.
Return to the title
Use the Title screen button or the Agromate logo. Your farm is saved before returning to the menu.

You start with 10 parsnip seeds. Seeds in the shop are sold in packs of five. An unwatered crop waits until it receives care; it does not grow overnight.

Orders, improvements and your workhorse

The order board reserves requested crops from sell-all. Deliver them explicitly for the displayed gold reward; completed offers return the next day. Orders never expire.

Buy permanent improvements at the Seed & tool shop. The copper watering can (350g) previews self, right and below, charging 1 energy for each newly watered crop. A sprinkler (600g) marks six covered tiles in cyan and waters at dawn, after growth, for the following night.

A stable with a horse (2500g) lets you name and ride your workhorse at 1.4× walking speed. Stand nearby and press M, or use Mount below the farm. A plow (750g) tills up to three clear tiles in your current row while mounted for 2 energy. Press P or use its button. One optional carrot (F or Feed) reduces plowing to 1 energy until sleep, without stacking or feeding penalties. Dismounting needs clear adjacent ground.

A jam maker (900g) consumes one strawberry and finishes one jam after one sleep. Collect it in the shop and sell it with the basket for 180g. Loading does not advance processing. All 28 garden plots remain available.

A few things to know

Is Agromate free?

Yes. The game is free to play in your browser. It does not require an account, installation, or payment.

Where is my farm saved?

Your farmer’s name and farm progress are saved automatically in this browser’s local storage. Return using the same browser and device to continue. Guest saves stay on that browser. If account sign-in is configured, Account & cloud offers an optional private cloud slot with explicit import and load choices. Guest and cloud farms stay separate. Clearing site data or using a private browsing session can remove your progress. Local storage must be available for automatic saving.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. You can tap garden plots and tools and use the on-screen movement buttons. JavaScript and a modern web browser are required.

Can I play with friends?

Agromate is currently single-player. You can share the game’s link with friends, but farms are not shared between players.

Can I reduce the animation?

Yes. Agromate respects your device or browser’s reduced-motion preference. Optional ambient sound can be toggled in the game header.

How can I get in touch?

Use the Contact button in the game footer. The email address is revealed after you choose to contact us.
